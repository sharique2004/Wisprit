# category: homophones
#
# The class where the acoustics carry no information at all. "Their" and "there"
# are the same sound; only the sentence decides. This is the category that moves
# when a language model gets better and does not move when a microphone does, so
# it is the cleanest separator between the two kinds of improvement.
#
# No `terms=` anywhere: the whole utterance is the assertion. A single wrong
# word here is a real error a user would have to go back and fix, which is
# exactly what the zero-edit rate is supposed to catch.
#
# Pace: normal. Do NOT put a helpful stress on the target word — the sentence has
# to do the work, because in real dictation it is the only thing that will.
# Distance: 20-30 cm.

They said their flight lands at noon, but they're not sure the connection holds.
There are two of them over there, and their names are on the list.
It's been running for three days and its memory footprint has not moved.
We sent two of them to the warehouse too, and the rest went out today.
The principal reason we chose it was the principle of least surprise.
That was a lovely compliment, but it does not complement the rest of the design.
Please be discreet about it until the two discrete phases are both finished.
The change did not affect the outcome, so the effect was smaller than we feared.
Whose turn is it, and who's actually going to write the summary?
You're welcome to bring your laptop if you want to follow along.
We will go ahead whether or not the weather improves by Thursday.
The printer is stationary, and we are completely out of stationery again.
